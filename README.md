# 🌍 GreenMinds KE Charity Drive

GreenMinds KE is a community-led charity dedicated to environmental conservation, education support, and youth mentorship in Kenya.

---

## 🚀 Our Initiatives & Impact Timeline

### 🌳 1. Environmental Conservation (Tree Planting)
We organize regional tree-planting campaigns to combat local deforestation and promote climate action.
<br>
<img src="images/tree-planting.jpg" width="600" alt="Tree Planting Drive">

### 📚 2. Educational Support (A4 Book Donations)
Providing critical writing materials to primary and secondary school students to keep their education continuous.
<br>
<img src="images/book-donations.jpg" width="600" alt="A4 Exercise Books Donation">

### 🩺 3. Welfare & Health (Sanitary Pad Donations)
Empowering young girls by distributing essential hygiene products, ensuring they never miss school due to a lack of basic needs.
<br>
<img src="images/pad-donations.jpg" width="600" alt="Sanitary Pad Distribution">

### 📢 4. Youth Mentorship (Career Talks)
Delivering actionable career guidance, academic mentorship, and soft-skills sessions to high school students.
<br>
<img src="images/career-talks.jpg" width="600" alt="Career Mentorship Talk">
